Hierarchical clustering represents a dataset through a sequence of nested groups. An ultrametric is a distance function that encodes such a hierarchy, making ultrametric approximation a natural way to study hierarchical representations of distance data.

I am interested in algorithms for these approximation problems and their connections to tree approximation and correlation clustering. My work with Anna C. Gilbert develops a hierarchical correlation clustering algorithm as part of an approach to fitting trees to distances.
