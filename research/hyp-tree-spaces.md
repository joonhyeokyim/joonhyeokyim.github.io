A tree provides a simple representation of distances and relationships. My work asks when a more general metric space admits an accurate tree approximation, and how to construct one efficiently.

Hyperbolicity measures how closely distances resemble those of a tree. I am particularly interested in average notions of hyperbolicity and in bounds that relate them to average approximation error. These questions connect the geometry of metric spaces with algorithms for fitting trees to distance data.
