Random graph models provide a setting for studying how local connections produce global geometric structure. I am interested in distances between typical vertices and in the shapes of geodesic triangles formed by shortest paths.

My work considers hyperbolicity, slimness, and minsize, including their average behavior in Erdős–Rényi and random regular graphs. These quantities capture different aspects of tree-like geometry and help distinguish typical behavior from worst-case configurations.
