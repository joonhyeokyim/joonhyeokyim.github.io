I am a mathematician interested in random graphs, metric geometry, and algorithms. My research studies geometric structure in graphs and metric spaces, including hyperbolicity, tree approximation, and hierarchical clustering.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from [Yale University](https://math.yale.edu/) in 2025, advised by [Anna C. Gilbert](https://annacgilbert.github.io/). I subsequently worked with her as a postdoctoral associate in Yale's Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering (2025–2026). I received my B.Sc. in Mathematical Sciences from Seoul National University in 2016.
